⬇ Download See features

Everything a duel server needs

Drop the jar in your mods folder and it's ready. Vanilla clients join as normal.

⚔

Challenges

Pick a player, kit, map and rounds from a menu. They get a big title and clickable Accept / Deny.

🏆

Ranked queue

Matchmaking by closest ELO, with ranks from Bronze to Champion, win streaks and a leaderboard.

🗺

Auto-built maps

Every duel gets its own arena far from spawn. Maps are reused, so rounds reset in milliseconds.

📦

Shulker kits

Fill a shulker, hold it, run /duel kit save name. Armor and offhand are equipped automatically.

✎

Kit layouts

Arrange each kit's hotbar once and it's used in every duel.

🏗

Your own maps

Build an arena, mark two corners and two spawns, save it. It's copied into every duel.

📊

Live HUD

Opponent health bar, sidebar with score pips, timer, pops and damage, plus combo counters.

👁

Spectating

Watch any live duel from the menu, with your own spectator scoreboard.

💾

Safe & persistent

Inventories are restored after every duel, even on disconnect or server stop. Stats and history are saved.

Chest menus

Commands

Most players only need /duel. Everything else is in the menu.

/duelOpen the menu
/duel <player>Challenge someone (menu picks kit, map, rounds)
/duel <player> <kit> <firstTo> <map>Challenge directly
/duel accept | deny [player]Answer a challenge
/duel queue <kit>Join ranked matchmaking
/duel rematchRun it back
/duel spectate <player>Watch a duel
/duel leaveForfeit or stop spectating
/duel stats | top | historyELO, leaderboard, recent duels
/duel layout <kit>Arrange a kit's hotbar
/duel kit save <name>Hold a shulker to make a custom kit
/duel arena pos1 | pos2 | spawn1 | spawn2Mark your own map
/duel arena save <name>Save the marked map

Download

Version 1.3.1 for Minecraft 26.2 and newer.

Fabric

Needs Fabric Loader 0.19+ and Fabric API.

⬇ Fabric jar

Forge

Needs Forge 65.1+.

⬇ Forge jar

Install

  1. Put the jar in your server's mods folder
  2. Start the server
  3. Type /duel in game

Settings live in config/duelarena/config.json.
